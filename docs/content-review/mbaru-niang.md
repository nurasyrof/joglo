## 1. Fixes needed

None found so far. The biggest gap is that **no height is sourced**: see section 2. The standard reference, Antar (2010), *Pesan dari Wae Rebo*, has measured drawings and was not available.

## 2. Model basis

One village, Wae Rebo. From M1 we take:

| In M1 | Value | In the model |
|---|---|---|
| Drum house diameter | 14 m | 14 m |
| Other houses | 11 m | 11 m |
| Houses | 1 mbaru gendang + 6 mbaru gena | Same |
| Position of the drum house | In the middle, facing the compang, three gena on each side | Same |
| Posts on the living floor | 1 worok + 8 mindi | 1 centre post + ring of 8 |
| Posts of levels 2–5 | 16 | 16 (two rings of 8) |
| Roof frame | 116 bamboo + 1 worok | 116 ribs + the centre post |
| Foundation | Posts wrapped in ijuk (24 + 24 trunks in the drum house) | 24 posts with a fibre wrap 📐 |
| Levels | 5 | 5 |
| Ngando | 3 bundles of ijuk; on the drum house, buffalo horn tied to the main post | Same |
| Gong | Hung in front of the drum house door | Same |

Our own estimates 📐, with nothing behind them but photographs in general:

- **Heights.** Drum house peak +15 m, the others +12.5 m. Living floor +1 m; upper floors at about +3.8, 6.2, 8.4 and 10.4 m in the drum house. Eaves 0.75 m above the ground.
- **Roof shape.** A slightly bulging cone.
- **Village circle.** Houses 32 m from the compang, at 0°, ±31°, ±59° and ±87°. Compang 7–9 m across and 1 m high.
- **Rooms.** Eight in the drum house and six in the others, to match the number of families (M3), in an arc of 230° round the back. M1 counts room posts and partition boards but gives no plan.
- **Entrance.** Four steps (M1 lists eight treads), door 1.1 × 1.8 m, roof lifted to +3.9 m.
- **Hearth.** One, just behind the centre post, with a four-post rack (M1: four planks, four rack posts).

## 4. Open questions

1. Heights and level spacing: Antar (2010) has sections of the rebuilt houses.
2. The real plan of the living floor: where the rooms, hearth and ladder are, and how many rooms.
3. The real positions of the seven houses. Wae Rebo's circle is not regular, and there are other buildings (guest houses, a kitchen hut) that we leave out.
4. Spelling: lempa rae / lampe rae / lemparai.
5. Whether the centre post really runs unbroken to the peak.
6. Zulkarnain et al. (2024), on the structural elements and joints, was blocked; it should give the names of the beams and the "three beams" M2 mentions.
