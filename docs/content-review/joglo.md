## 1. Fixes needed

All three fixed on 3 October 2026 (F1 also reworded the About text; F2 renamed the part id to `lambang_gantung`).

| # | Where | Problem | Proposed fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 ✔ | About ¶3; Pendapa · Umpak (fn); Dalem · Umpak (fn) | ❌ We say the frame "rides out earthquakes" and "can shift and settle instead of snapping". A survey of 20 joglo damaged in the 2006 Yogyakarta earthquake (S3) found **slip between columns and their stone bases** as one of the main damage types, a weak outer frame, and collapses in the worst cases. The core frame (saka guru + tumpang sari) held up better than the outer rings. | About ¶3 → "The frame is held together by timber joints rather than nails and rests on stone bases, so it can be taken apart and rebuilt elsewhere. In the 2006 Yogyakarta earthquake the stiff core around the four saka guru generally held up better than the lighter outer frame." Umpak fn → drop the earthquake sentence; keep "keeps the teak dry and away from termites". |
| F2 ✔ | Pendapa · "Lambang Sari"; Dalem · "Blandar, Sunduk & Lambang Sari" | ❌ What we model (a gap between brunjung and penanggap, held by short hanging posts) is a **lambang gantung**, carried by **saka benthung**. A **lambangsari** is the opposite: a beam that joins the two tiers *without* a gap (S1, S1b). | Rename the part to **Lambang Gantung** (alias *Saka benthung*): "Short hanging posts (saka benthung) at the foot of the brunjung carry the penanggap roof, leaving a gap between the two tiers that lets warm air out and light in." Meaning → "Joglo are classified by how the tiers meet: in the lambang gantung type there is a gap, in the lambangsari type a beam joins them directly." Dalem part → "Blandar, Sunduk & Lambang Gantung". |
| F3 ✔ | Pendapa · Saka Penanggap (alias) | ❔ Alias "saka pengarak" not found in any source. | Remove the alias (use "Saka penanggap" alone). |

## 4. Our own interpretations (💭)

These "meaning" lines are interpretations written for the site, not taken from a source. They are flagged `interp: true` in the part data, so the site shows them with a violet **Interpretation** label. Remove the flag once a source is found.

- Sunduk & Kili: "reflects the Javanese ideal of harmony (rukun)"
- Usuk & Reng: "a picture of gotong royong"
- Atap Brunjung: "a roof like a mountain (gunungan)"
- Umpak: "the padma motif suggests purity rising out of the ground"
- Saka Penanggap: "like attendants around the four masters"
- Dhadha Peksi: "strong, central and protective"
- Uleng: "its depth marks the sacred centre"

## 5. Model dimensions (📐)

All sizes, heights, pitches and counts in `specs` (e.g. "16.6 × 14.6 m", "≈52–57° pitch", "36 bases") describe this model, which is an idealised compound. They are not claims about any real house. The About text already says so.
